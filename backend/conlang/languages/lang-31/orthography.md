# Ortografia

Nome da língua: **Akixontaqian** — nativamente *akixontaq* /akiʃontaŋ/, literalmente 'person'

- Estilo: econômico (letras reaproveitadas: x, c, q)
- Separador para sequências ambíguas: `·` (9 sequências)

## Frases

- **Iimyaki hulhuuwo.** /iːmjaki huɬuːwo/ — 'The child sleeps.'
- **Ozwuu twukuu ukwu.** /ozwuː twukuː ukwu/ — 'The woman sees the fish.'
- **Plaa twukuumi alenuupu.** /plaː twukuːmi alenuːpu/ — 'The dog ate the fish.'
- **Ozwuu iimyakika twukuu yirlĩ.** /ozwuː iːmjakika twukuː jirlĩ/ — 'The woman gives the fish to the child.'
- **Ka gwiny ukwu.** /ka ɡwiɲ ukwu/ — 'I see you.'
- **Plaami twaa ogwu twu yapil.** /plaːmi twaː oɡwu twu japil/ — 'These two big dogs come.'
- **Ozwuun plaa hulhuuwo.** /ozwuːn plaː huɬuːwo/ — 'The woman's dog sleeps.'
- **Iimyaki klaxuutyaaun hulhuuwo.** /iːmjaki klaʃuːtjaːun huɬuːwo/ — 'The child sleeps in the house.'
- **Twukuu twaa.** /twukuː twaː/ — 'The fish is big.'
- **Iimyaki hulhuuwogu.** /iːmjaki huɬuːwoɡu/ — 'The child does not sleep.'
- **Ozwuu twukuu ukwu li.** /ozwuː twukuː ukwu li/ — 'Does the woman see the fish?'
- **Twa ozwuu ukwu.** /twa ozwuː ukwu/ — 'What does the woman see?'
