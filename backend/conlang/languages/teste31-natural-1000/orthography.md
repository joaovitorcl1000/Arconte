# Ortografia

- Estilo: econômico (letras reaproveitadas: x, c, q)
- Separador para sequências ambíguas: `·` (11 sequências)

## Frases

- **Am kwip.** /am kwip/ — 'The child sleeps.'
- **Tyul bi alqo.** /tjul bi alŋo/ — 'The woman sees the fish.'
- **Syun bimi upyonpu.** /sjun bimi upjonpu/ — 'The dog ate the fish.'
- **Tyul amka bi maam.** /tjul amka bi maːm/ — 'The woman gives the fish to the child.'
- **Ka gwiny alqo.** /ka ɡwiɲ alŋo/ — 'I see you.'
- **Syunmi yaap ul ar klaam.** /sjunmi jaːp ul ar klaːm/ — 'These two big dogs come.'
- **Tyun syun kwip.** /tjun sjun kwip/ — 'The woman's dog sleeps.'
- **Am gwaaqumun kwip.** /am ɡwaːŋumun kwip/ — 'The child sleeps in the house.'
- **Bi yaap.** /bi jaːp/ — 'The fish is big.'
- **Am kwipgu.** /am kwipɡu/ — 'The child does not sleep.'
- **Tyul bi alqo tyum.** /tjul bi alŋo tjum/ — 'Does the woman see the fish?'
- **Klu tyul alqo.** /klu tjul alŋo/ — 'What does the woman see?'
