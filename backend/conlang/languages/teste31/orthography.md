# Ortografia

- Estilo: econômico (letras reaproveitadas: x, c, q)
- Separador para sequências ambíguas: `·` (9 sequências)

## Frases

- **Am lulim.** /am lulim/ — 'The child sleeps.'
- **Tyulĩ bi alqo.** /tjulĩ bi alŋo/ — 'The woman sees the fish.'
- **Syun bimi upyonpu.** /sjun bimi upjonpu/ — 'The dog ate the fish.'
- **Tyulĩ amka bi maam.** /tjulĩ amka bi maːm/ — 'The woman gives the fish to the child.'
- **Ka gwiny alqo.** /ka ɡwiɲ alŋo/ — 'I see you.'
- **Syunmi hukla ul ar klaam.** /sjunmi hukla ul ar klaːm/ — 'These two big dogs come.'
- **Tyulĩn syun lulim.** /tjulĩn sjun lulim/ — 'The woman's dog sleeps.'
- **Am gwaaqumun lulim.** /am ɡwaːŋumun lulim/ — 'The child sleeps in the house.'
- **Bi hukla.** /bi hukla/ — 'The fish is big.'
- **Am lulimgu.** /am lulimɡu/ — 'The child does not sleep.'
- **Tyulĩ bi alqo tyum.** /tjulĩ bi alŋo tjum/ — 'Does the woman see the fish?'
- **Twu tyulĩ alqo.** /twu tjulĩ alŋo/ — 'What does the woman see?'
